KnightBall is a two-player abstract strategy board game (original rules, not a chess variant clone).

Quick verification path:
1. Tap "Play Now" - a game vs the on-device AI starts immediately, no account needed. Pieces move like chess knights; drag or tap a blue piece, then a highlighted square.
2. Offline play: games against the AI work fully offline (try Airplane Mode). The neural-network AI runs on-device (WebGL + tree search) and all 15 difficulty levels ship inside the app.
3. Sign-in (Login button, top right) is optional: email/username + password (use the demo account above), or "Email me a sign-in link" (one-time magic link). There is no third-party sign-in.
4. Online multiplayer (requires network): signed in or anonymous, Menu > New Game > Online.
5. Account deletion (guideline 5.1.1(v)): user menu (avatar, top right) > Delete Account.

When online, finished on-device games are uploaded to knightball.org for game history and AI training. Support: https://knightball.org/support
