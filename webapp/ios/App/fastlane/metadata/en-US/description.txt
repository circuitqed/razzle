Ever wonder what chess knights do when they're not defending their king?

They play ball.

KnightBall is a two-player abstract strategy game: five knights, one ball, one goal. Move like chess knights, pass the ball in straight lines, and be the first to get your ball to the far row.

Simple rules, deep tactics:
- Knights move exactly like chess knights
- The ball passes along ranks, files, and diagonals between your knights
- A knight that has not moved yet cannot receive a pass, so plan your development
- First ball to reach the opponent's back row wins

Play your way:
- 15 AI difficulty levels, from first-game friendly to master strength, powered by an AlphaZero-style neural network that runs entirely on your device
- Online multiplayer: quick match, or invite a friend with a join code
- Follow the game with the live evaluation meter
- Step through finished games move by move

No ads. Sign in with Apple, Google, or email - or just play without an account.
